From July 8-10, 2026, the SmartGit team was part of WeAreDevelopers Berlin.
Under the motto **"Your Git Mission Starts Here"** our booth became a meeting point for developers who wanted to talk about Git workflows, repository challenges, and smarter ways to work with Git.

We met long-time SmartGit users, first-time visitors, students, teams, and developers from many different technical backgrounds. The conversations ranged from everyday Git questions to complex branching strategies, merge conflicts, worktrees, and repository history.

### The SmartGit Features Visitors Asked About

<strong><a href="https://www.smartgit.dev/features/visual-history/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Visual History</a></strong>
Complex commit histories can quickly become difficult to understand. SmartGit's customizable graph helps developers follow branches, merges, tags, and individual commits more clearly. 

<strong><a href="https://www.smartgit.dev/features/smart-branching/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Smart Branching</a></strong><br> 
SmartGit supports different branching strategies and helps users manage branches visually. Features such as Feature-Flow make it easier to follow structured workflows without losing control of the underlying Git operations. 

<strong><a href="https://www.smartgit.dev/features/conflict-resolution/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Conflict Solver</a></strong><br> 
The three-pane Conflict Solver shows both versions of a conflict and the resulting file. This makes it easier to compare changes and resolve conflicts with confidence. 

<strong>Commit Overlap</strong><br>
Complex Git histories can quickly become unclear. SmartGit's Commit Overlap reveals related changes, highlights candidates for reordering or squashing, and helps you spot potential conflicts earlier. <strong><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p84uBgYndS4" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Watch the Commit Overlap Video</a></strong>
