The first public preview build of **SmartSVN 14.6** is available for download.

The most important new features/improvements are:

- Changes view:
  - Word wrap support
  - improved look
  - more appealing colors
  - improved inner-line changes
- reworked, theme-dependent icons
- File Compare, Conflict Solver:
  - improved colors and look
- Preferences dialog: opens much faster
- support for MacOS 27
