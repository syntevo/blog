SmartGit is now available with its full feature set free of charge for your own unpaid, non-commercial hobby projects.

[Download](https://www.smartgit.dev/download/)

## A professional Git client at zero cost for your personal, non-commercial projects.
SmartGit helps you see your branches, commits, and changes clearly - whether you are building a personal app, learning Git, experimenting with a new technology, or working on a game.

And now, your Hobby License gives you more freedom.

Starting with **SmartGit 26.1.050**, the Hobby License includes the full feature set (*except enterprise integrations*). 

You can now:
- **Use SmartGit with any repository** for your eligible hobby projects.
- **Use the Standard Window** for your everyday Git workflow.
- **Use repositories without cloud-based unlocking.**

This gives you more flexibility in how you work and more tools to help you understand and manage your project.

## Free licenses for other non-commercial uses
A Hobby License is one of several ways to use SmartGit free of charge. Other categories include:

- **Students & education**: Students and employees of public educational institutions can apply with their educational email address.
- **Open-source development**: Developers who spend the majority of their development work on eligible open-source projects can apply. Occasional unpaid contributors may qualify for a Hobby License.
- **Charitable organizations**: Eligible public charitable organizations working in philanthropy, health research, education, or social well-being can apply.

Each category has its own eligibility requirements and verification. [Find the right free license for your use case.](https://www.smartgit.dev/pricing/)
